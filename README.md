# 🌸 FNP Sales Analysis — Excel Dashboard Project

> **An end-to-end data analysis project on Ferns and Petals (FNP) sales data using Microsoft Excel — covering revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, and occasion-based insights through an interactive dashboard.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Ferns and Petals (FNP)** is one of India's leading gifting companies, delivering gifts for occasions like Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries.

This project analyzes FNP's internal sales dataset to answer **10 critical business questions** and presents findings through a fully interactive **Excel Dashboard** with slicers, timelines, pivot charts, and KPI cards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify overall revenue and key performance metrics
- Analyze sales trends across months and occasions
- Determine top-performing products and categories
- Understand customer spending behavior
- Find top cities by order volume
- Examine relationship between order quantity and delivery time

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Colors** is the highest revenue category at **₹10,05,645 (28.6%)** of total revenue
- **February** (Valentine's Day) and **August** (Raksha Bandhan) are the strongest sales months
- **Tier-2 cities** like Imphal, Dhanbad, and Kavali show surprisingly high order volumes
- **Anniversary** drives the highest occasion-specific revenue at ₹6,74,634
- **Male vs Female** spending is nearly equal — ₹17,90,223 vs ₹17,30,761
- **Diwali** underperforms relative to its cultural significance — a growth opportunity
- Top 3 categories (Colors, Soft Toys, Sweets) together account for **70%+ of total revenue**

## 💡 Recommendations

1. **Stock up Colors, Soft Toys & Sweets** — they drive 70%+ of revenue
2. **Boost Diwali & Valentine's campaigns** — both occasions currently underperform
3. **Expand to Tier-2 cities** — Imphal, Dhanbad, Kavali show strong demand
4. **Create occasion-specific bundles** (e.g., Holi Color Kits, Diwali Sweet Hampers)
5. **Pre-season promotions in Jan & Apr** to offset low-revenue months
6. **Maintain delivery SLA under 5 days** for better customer satisfaction
